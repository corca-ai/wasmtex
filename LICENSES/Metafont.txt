GFtoPK is by Tomas Rokicki, and `gftopk.web` states no terms of its own. The
TeX Live package database records `mfware`, the package that ships it, as public
domain (`catalogue-license pd`), and this release relies on that record.

The WasmTex build does not edit either master file. It applies the Web2C change
files of the pinned TeX Live source and adds a screen stub and a file-lookup
interposition layer. The result is distributed as `wasmtex-metafont`, not as an
unmodified upstream binary. The exact masters, change files, generated C, glue, and
build scripts are provided in the corresponding-source archive.
