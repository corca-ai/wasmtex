The WasmTex build does not edit the `bibtex.web` master file. It applies the
Web2C change-file build used by the pinned TeX Live source and adds a separate
WebAssembly entry point and file-lookup interposition layer. The resulting
executable is distributed as `wasmtex-bibtex`, not as an unmodified upstream
binary. The exact master, change files, generated C, glue, and build scripts
are provided in the corresponding-source archive.
